Dealkalizer

A chloride-cycle dealkalizer after the softener removes most of the alkalinity. That raises the cycles the boiler can run and cuts the CO₂ that ends up in the condensate.

The resin trades sulfate, nitrate and bicarbonate for chloride, so all three use capacity: load = M alk + SO₄ × 1.042 + NO₃ × 0.807 as CaCO₃. Conductivity barely changes, so after a dealkalizer silica or conductivity usually becomes the cycles limit. Condensate treatment savings scale with makeup alkalinity load.

Reverse osmosis

Flash steam = blowdown × (hf,boiler − hf,flash) ÷ hfg,flash. Exchanger heat = drain lb × (hin − (Tout − 32)). Uses today's blowdown; if a cycles project goes in first, there is less to recover.

Condensate return

Returning more condensate saves its heat, the makeup water and chemicals that replace it, and blowdown, since less makeup brings in fewer solids.

Boiler water concentration stays the same, so cycles rise as makeup falls: Cnew = (C(1 − CR) + CR − CRnew) ÷ (1 − CRnew). Heat saved per lb of returned condensate = condensate °F − makeup °F.
